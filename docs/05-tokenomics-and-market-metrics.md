# 05 · Tokenomics & Market Metrics

## Tokenomics (token economics)

Tokenomics refers to the economic design, behavior, and incentives surrounding a cryptocurrency token or blockchain-based project. It's one of the most important aspects to analyze when evaluating the long-term potential and value of a crypto project.

**Key elements**

1. Token supply
2. Token distribution
3. Token utility
4. Inflation / deflation
5. **Vesting schedule**: controls when tokens held by insiders (team, early investors) become available. It helps prevent "pump and dump" behavior.
6. **Market behavior influences**: slippage, liquidity, market cap, and volatility.

## Market cap (capitalization)

The total value of the coins in circulation.

```
Market cap = circulating supply × current price
```

**Why it matters**
- **Measures size & ranking**: higher market cap = bigger coin = higher rank (e.g. BTC > ETH > SOL).
- **Risk indicator**:
  - High market cap = more stable, less volatile (e.g. BTC, ETH)
  - Low market cap = more volatile, higher potential reward/risk (e.g. new memecoins)

## Types of supply

**Circulating supply** is the number of coins or tokens currently available and actively circulating in the market, meaning they can be bought, sold, or traded by the public.

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Circulating supply** | Coins that are in the market and available for trading |
| **Total supply** | All coins ever created, minus those burned |
| **Max supply** | The maximum number of coins that will ever exist (e.g. BTC = 21 million) |
| **Locked supply** | Coins that are not available yet (e.g. team-held, vesting, staking, etc.) |

## FDV (Fully Diluted Valuation)

First, know that price is related to multiple factors: **time, supply, demand**.

```
FDV = price × max supply
```

When you look at the FDV, ask these questions:
- What is the current market cap?
- When do emissions end?
- What was the past demand?
- What could the future demand be?

If a token's FDV is much greater than its market cap, avoid it. There is a gap between circulating supply and max supply (locked tokens), which means that when they unlock the price may go down (depending on the project).
